Is my donation secure?

Yes. Payments are handled by Localgiving, a UK platform for community organisations. We never see your card details.

Can I claim Gift Aid?

Haven of Light is a Community Interest Company (CIC), a not-for-profit company that exists for community benefit. CICs are not registered charities, so donations to us aren't eligible for Gift Aid.
