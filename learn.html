Education

Learning that leads to action.

We believe in lifelong learning, formal and informal. Whether you're a teacher, a minister, a community leader or simply curious, start here.

How learning works with us

1

Discover

Hear the facts and real stories behind an issue, at the right level for your group.

2

Understand

Explore the causes: poverty, conflict, inequality and the systems that let exploitation happen.

3

Reflect

Reset your moral compass. What do I value, and where do I have influence?

4

Act

Leave with one practical step: what to buy, who to tell, how to welcome, how to report.

Churches and faith communities

Faith that stands in the gap.

People of goodwill and those with a living faith have a significant part to play. Churches are often the first place a vulnerable person finds welcome, and congregations can change how a whole community sees injustice.

We have worked alongside the Church in Wales and the Diocese of St Asaph, including a Guide to Modern Slavery for the Church Community.

12 spheres of influence

Each of us lives within areas of life where we meet others: family, community, work and more. Rooted in faith, values, beliefs and traditions, this project looks at 12 of them as places where good things can happen.
