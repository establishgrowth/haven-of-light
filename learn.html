Churches and faith communities

Faith that stands in the gap.

People of goodwill and those with a living faith have a significant part to play. Churches are often the first place a vulnerable person finds welcome, and congregations can change how a whole community sees injustice.

We have worked alongside the Church in Wales and the Diocese of St Asaph, including a Guide to Modern Slavery for the Church Community.

Project

12 spheres of influence

Everyone has the potential to make a real difference!

Starting from faith and values, beliefs and traditions, we look at 12 spheres of influence. These are areas of life where we find ourselves, and where we can be part of transformational change.

Some spheres are large, like family or community. Others overlap. Each one is a place where good things can happen.

Hover over or tap a sphere to explore it.

Sphere 01 of 12

Global Justice & Human Rights

Standing up for the dignity and freedom of every person, near and far, including people trapped in modern slavery and exploitation.

One step you can take

Learn the signs of exploitation and how to report a concern.

Sphere 02 of 12

Family

Where values are first learnt and lived. A home can be a place of safety, belonging and kindness, where children learn to care about others.

One step you can take

Talk together about fairness, and about where the things you buy come from.

Sphere 03 of 12

Community

Neighbourhoods, churches and local groups where we meet, welcome and look out for one another.

One step you can take

Get to know someone new in your area, especially someone who may be isolated.

Sphere 04 of 12

Education & Learning

Schools, colleges and lifelong learning shape how people see the world and their own part in it.

One step you can take

Invite Haven of Light to run a session with your class or group.

Sphere 05 of 12

Medicine & Healthcare

Health workers often meet people no one else sees, including victims of exploitation. Good care is a right for everyone.

One step you can take

If you work in healthcare, learn the referral routes for suspected exploitation.

Sphere 06 of 12

Sport & Leisure

Teams, clubs and free time build friendship, confidence and fair play, and can include people who are often left out.

One step you can take

Ask how your club could welcome newcomers and refugees.

Sphere 07 of 12

Arts & Entertainment

Stories, music and art move hearts and help us understand lives very different from our own.

One step you can take

Use a film, song or poem to start a conversation about justice.

Sphere 08 of 12

Environment

Caring for creation, and for the people hit hardest when land, water and climate are damaged.

One step you can take

Choose products that respect both people and planet.

Sphere 09 of 12

Government & State

Laws, policies and public services that protect rights and hold power to account.

One step you can take

Write to your MS or MP about an issue you care about.

Sphere 10 of 12

Communication & Media

What we read, watch and share shapes how we see others. Media can expose injustice or spread prejudice.

One step you can take

Check your sources, and share stories that build understanding.

Sphere 11 of 12

Economics & Business

Work, wages and supply chains. Business can lift people out of poverty, or exploit them.

One step you can take

Ask the companies you buy from how they keep forced labour out of their supply chains.

Sphere 12 of 12

Science & Technology

Innovation solves problems, but technology can also be misused, including for online grooming and trafficking.

One step you can take

Learn how to stay safe online, and help young people do the same.
